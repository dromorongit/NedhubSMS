About the Blacklist

Numbers here will never receive SMS messages from your account.

How it works

  • Added numbers are normalized (e.g. 0241234567 becomes +233 24 123 4567) so they match regardless of format.
  • Blacklisted numbers are excluded from all SMS campaigns and message sends.
  • Use a reason when adding a number, and an optional custom reason for extra detail.
  • Remove a number at any time to allow messages to it again.
